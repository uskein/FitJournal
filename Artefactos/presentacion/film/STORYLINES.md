# STORYLINES — DiarioFit launch film (36 s · 1920×1080 · 30 fps)

Source of truth for copy and facts: `Artefactos/documentacion/Producto y Flujos/REQUERIMIENTOS_FUNCIONALES.md`
(Bitácora de vida + gimnasio, local-first, SQLite, sin roles de administrador) and the eight real
captures in `assets/screens/` taken from the running APK v1.1.0.

---

## RECOMMENDED — A · "Un día cualquiera"

**Device:** a real day walked through the product — the day is the spine and the streak is the
one piece of data that travels from hook to payoff.
**Form:** object-to-object chain — every shot ends on the thing that opens the next (the streak
card → the day it belongs to → the habit under it → the routine → the record it produced → the
calendar that holds them all).

The viewer opens on a real "Hoy" that is already working (14 días seguidos, energía 4/5), feels
two honest frustrations named by the project's own personas, then watches four verbs performed
in the real UI: *escribe, marca, registra, mira*. It closes on the month filling with mood colour
— the product's most own image — and lands the mark. Calm, warm, earned; the film a stranger
follows because it is one day, not a feature list.

- **First two seconds:** the real Hoy screen rising into a cream frame, streak card already lit,
  kicker `DIARIOFIT · LOCAL-FIRST` above it; no black, no logo alone.
- **Beats:** hook → tension (two persona frustrations as type) → reveal (wordmark) →
  proof (escribe / marca / registra / mira, one verb each in real UI) → payoff (calendar fills)
  → claim → end card (icon carried in, wordmark wipes, CTA held).
- **Look:** surface `#FAF6F0` cream ground, clay shadow under every elevated plane, Fraunces
  display + Nunito Sans body + JetBrains Mono for every number; the real screenshots are the only
  imagery, cropped and pushed to 1.6–2× their real size.
- **Sound:** warm minimal pulse ≈100 bpm that opens when the product appears; soft key ticks on
  the four verbs, one riser into the calendar payoff, short fade under the end card.

**Why recommended:** it is built entirely from captures that already exist, so every frame is
product truth, and the chain form gives each transition a cause instead of a cut.

## B · "El número" (type carries the argument)

**Device:** a number lands — the streak, earned by a scene, then made physical.
**Form:** giant kinetic type and colour fields cut on the beat; `14` in JetBrains Mono grows out
of the day's log while the calendar assembles behind it.

- **First two seconds:** frame 0 is a cream field with a single huge `14` and the kicker
  `días seguidos` — poster, not product.
- **Beats:** hook (the number) → tension (it is easy to break) → reveal → proof (the four verbs
  as flashes) → payoff (the number becomes the month grid) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** same palette, but type-first: Fraunces at display scale, fewer screens, one accent.
- **Sound:** percussive, cut to the beat, one impact on the payoff.

**Dropped because:** a launch built mostly on type shows less of the product than a viewer needs
to believe it.

## C · "Antes / después"

**Device:** life before and after — the scattered old way, then one place for it.
**Form:** split screen collapsing to one; the old side churns, the new side moves calmly.

- **First two seconds:** two honest frustrations from the personas typed on a churn field:
  *"Diarios que abandonas por no saber qué escribir"* / *"Apps lentas para anotar series"*.
- **Beats:** tension → reveal → proof (the same four verbs, now calm) → payoff → claim → end card.
- **Look:** muted `#F4EFE8` field on the old side, cream on the new; identical type.
- **Sound:** sparse and dry for the old side, the bed entering at the reveal.

**Dropped because:** the "before" has no real imagery in this project, so it would be carried by
type alone — which storyline B already does with a stronger single idea.
